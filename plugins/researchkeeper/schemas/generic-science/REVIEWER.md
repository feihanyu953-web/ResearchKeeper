# Generic Science Reviewer

Read the active modules, `INDEX.md`, `SCHEMA.md`, this file, each paper's
coverage declaration, the referenced module records, and the original paper
and SI.

Verify provenance, required fields, duplicates, conflicts, numeric accuracy,
and cross-module consistency. For `not_applicable`, independently confirm
that the cited paper or SI locations genuinely contain no applicable content.
Do not convert missing evidence into a pass.
